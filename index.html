About

Thomas is an undergraduate student at Worcester Polytechnic Institute pursuing a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering and graduating May 2027.

He is passionate about space exploration and technology, with a technical focus on space systems, Earth observation, remote sensing, GIS, and imaging systems. He is actively involved in projects spanning aerospace engineering, science, STEM outreach, and astronomy.
